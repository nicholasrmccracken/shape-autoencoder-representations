# Question 4: Autoencoder Tuning for Improved Shape Accuracy

My goal was to maximize the shape 1-NN accuracy by forcing the latent representation to prioritize geometry over color.

## Changes Made

1. **Forced Grayscale Input:** I converted the images to grayscale during training. This removed color information, forcing the network to just focus on reconstructing the essential structural features (i.e. edges and boundaries).

2. **Enforced Rotation Invariance:** I added random 180-degree rotation to the training augmentation. This forces the encoder to map the same shape, regardless of its angle, to a consistent point in the latent space, which directly improves the robustness of the shape feature, thus leading to higher shape identification accuracy, seen below. 

## Results

This table compares the 1-NN classification accuracy across each encoder layers for both the **Original Model** and the **Tuned Q4 Model**.

| Layer Name | **Original (Color)** | **Original (Shape)** | **Tuned Q4 (Color)** | **Tuned Q4 (Shape)** | **Accuracy Shift (%)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **pixels**  | 82.95% | 64.15% | 13.03% | 78.97% | **+14.82** |
| **layer_1** | 73.27% | 68.10% | 22.82% | 74.75% | **+6.65**  |
| **layer_2** | 66.78% | 67.98% | 16.65% | 80.08% | **+12.10** |
| **layer_3** | 63.60% | 66.37% | 16.40% | 84.87% | **+18.50** |
| **layer_4** | 53.35% | 70.78% | 18.03% | 86.20% | **+15.42** |
| **layer_5** | 47.42% | 73.87% | 18.10% | 87.27% | **+13.40** |
| **layer_6** | **39.90%** | **73.30%** | **20.80%** | **87.45%** | **+14.15** |

The final shape 1-NN accuracy for layer 6 on the test set was **87.45%**.

## Conclusion

The tuning strategy successfully increased the shape accuracy by **14.15%** points (from 73.30% to 87.45%) at the critical bottleneck layer. This significant increase proves the effectiveness of my grayscale and rotation strategies. The autoencoder was forced to ignore color, and dedicate all its latent space to encoding the shape of the objects. Consequently, the color accuracy of Q4 is nothing more than a guess with no color information available.
